Scale/Rotate

Once you initiate the Scale/Rotate mode, ASSDraw3 will display a rectangle that bounds the shape on canvas. You can then manipulate this bounding rectangle to scale and/or rotate the shape.


To scale the shape, point you mouse pointer to an edge (i.e. a side of the rectangle) or a vertex (i.e. a corner where two sides meet) until one or two circles appear on the bounding rectangle. Using the left mouse button, drag the edge/vertex to rescale the rectangle, and the shape will rescale to fill in the rectangle.

To rotate the shape, drag using your right mouse button. Note: you do not have to point on an edge or a vertex.
