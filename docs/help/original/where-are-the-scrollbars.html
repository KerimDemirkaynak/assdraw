Where are the scrollbars?

Nope, the canvas does not have scrollbars. Unlike a document, which has a definite dimension, the canvas in ASSDraw3 has infinite dimension, meaning you can draw shapes as large as you want as there is no such thing as the border of the canvas. Therefore a scrollbar does not make sense here.


Even though the canvas is virtually infinite, your viewpoint (i.e the screen) is not, thus ASSDraw3 can only show a limited portion of the whole infinite canvas. At times, some parts of the shape on the canvas do not fit in the viewpoint. To make it possible to view any or all parts of the shape within the viewpoint, ASSDraw3 provides you with two mechanisms:


  • Panning
    With panning mechanism, you move the shape around within the viewpoint by right-dragging (dragging using right mouse button) the canvas. If some portion on the right side of the shape is hidden, you can pan the canvas to the left, vice versa.

  • Zooming
    By default, ASSDraw3's canvas is zoomed in at 1000% at startup, meaning that the shape displayed on the canvas is in the scale of 10:1 of actual size rendered on video. If the shape appears too big that it is difficult to visualize how it looks like, you can zoom out to a lower zoom factor by using the mousewheel, or PageDown and Ctrl-PageDown if your mouse does not have a wheel. (PageUp or Ctrl-PageUp to zoom in)
