My canvas background shows black box where video frame should be displayed. Why?

Let me guess. You do the printscreen trick on your Media Player Classic (MPC) and directly paste into ASSDraw3. Tell you what, it is not something ASSDraw3 can do anything about. If you paste the screenshot to any image editor you will get the same black box. It is caused by how video is overlaid on screen by DirectShow. DirectShow renders the video directly onto video memory, bypassing Windows, thus when you do PrtSc, Windows captures the black box instead of the video frame because it can't see the latter.


To fix this, you need to open the Options dialog in MPC (sorry, can't help if you use something else) by pressing O key. Navigate to Playback > Output. In the DirectShow Video box, choose a "renderless" renderer. Click 'Apply' or 'OK' and re-open the video file (simply unpausing won't work). Do the PrtSc again.
