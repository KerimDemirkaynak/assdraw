How do I save the shape to a file?

You can't. Well, at least not in a straightforward way. You can manually save a shape you have designed by getting the drawing commands first, and later copy-pasting them into a text-editor such as Notepad and save as a .txt or anything at all.


Unless you are planning to send the drawing commands in an email or post them on file-sharing networks, ASSDraw's built-in Shapes Library might suffice to 'save' the shape you design.
